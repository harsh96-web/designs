Studio policies

Fair to you, fair to the chair.

Booking an appointment means you're happy with these. You can always ask me first.

Booking

Held just for you

Your booking reserves a full chair block in your name. A card on file secures it, and nothing is charged when you book. You only pay for the service you sit for.

Cancelling

48 hours, free

Reschedule or cancel for free up to 48 hours before your appointment. Later than that, or a no-show, a fee applies to the card on file, because a three-hour chair can't be refilled last minute.

Running late

A quick message saves it

Text me on WhatsApp if you're running behind. There's a short grace window. Past it, the service may need to be shortened or moved so the next client isn't kept waiting.

Before we start

We begin with a plan

New colour starts with a consultation and a short consent form at your first visit. It keeps the plan, the price and your hair's safety clear before anything begins.

Getting it right

Love it before you leave

We check your look together at the chair. If anything feels off, tell me before you leave and I'll perfect it the same day. Colour settles over the first week, so give it a few washes before judging the tone.

Offers

Same care, every booking

Promotional bookings get the same products and care as everything else. Results still depend on your hair's history and condition, so the consultation always comes first.

The adjustment promise

Colour is a craft, and I stand behind mine. One conversation fixes almost everything.

  1. Something feels off? Message me first.
  2. Prompt adjustments to the original service are free.
  3. A change of mind is a new service.
  4. Home care as advised keeps the promise valid.
Aftercare

Make it last.

Typical timings. I'll confirm yours at the chair. Had a colour correction? Follow the stage plan we set together.

Balayage

Refresh in 3–4 months
  1. First few daysWait a couple of days before the first wash. Judge the tone after a few washes.
  2. Every washSulphate-free, colour-safe shampoo, cooler water, heat protectant before hot tools.
  3. Weeks 4–6Toner softens. Purple or blue shampoo once or twice a week tops it up.

Highlights

Refresh in 6–8 weeks
  1. First few daysWait a couple of days before the first wash.
  2. Every washSulphate-free, colour-safe shampoo and heat protectant.
  3. Weeks 4–6Brass showing? Purple shampoo once or twice a week, never every wash.

All-over colour & roots

Touch-up in 4–6 weeks
  1. First few daysWait a couple of days before the first wash.
  2. Every washSulphate-free shampoo and cooler water. In summer, UV protection keeps the shade from fading.
  3. Want longer gaps?Ask about a softer, blended look that grows out gently.

Keratin smoothing

Redo in 3–5 months
  1. First few daysGo gently: no tight ponytails, clips or tucking hair behind your ears.
  2. Every washSulphate-free shampoo only. Sulphates and salt water strip smoothing faster.
  3. When to rebookFrizz creeping back is your sign it's time.

Hair botox

Repeat in 2–3 months
  1. Every washSulphate-free shampoo and a rich conditioner keep the gloss in longer.
  2. Once a weekA deep-conditioning mask keeps the repair going.
  3. When to rebookShine fading and ends feeling dry again.

Soft perm

Shape-up in ~3 months
  1. First 48 hoursKeep it dry and loose: no washing, tight ties or clips.
  2. Every washSulphate-free shampoo, light conditioner. Scrunch instead of brushing once dry.
  3. Around 3 monthsA shape-up trim keeps the curl sitting right.

Haircut

Trim in 6–10 weeks
  1. Every dayHeat protectant before hot tools, and detangle from the ends up.
  2. When to rebookSharper, shorter shapes want the earlier end.

Tape-in extensions

Move-up in 6–8 weeks
  1. First 48 hoursKeep the tapes dry and skip oils near the roots while they bond.
  2. Every dayBrush from the ends up with a soft or loop brush. Sleep in a loose braid.
