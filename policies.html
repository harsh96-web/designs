Policies & aftercare

Clear terms before you book, and simple care to keep your colour fresh. Questions? Just message me.

Booking policies

Booking
A card on file is required when you book. Any deposit goes toward your final service cost.
Cancelling or rescheduling
Please give at least 48 hours' notice. With 48 hours' notice, your deposit is refunded in full or moved to your new appointment. Cancellations within 48 hours forfeit the deposit.
Missed appointments
A no-show is subject to a fee of 50% of the service price, with any deposit credited toward it.
Running late
There's a 15-minute grace period for late arrivals. If you're running behind, message me.
If I need to cancel
Your deposit is refunded or moved to a new appointment.
Before we start
New colour starts with a consultation and a short consent form at your first visit, so the plan, the price and your hair's safety are clear first.
Getting it right
We check your hair together before you leave. If something feels off, tell me then and I'll adjust it the same day. Colour settles over the first week, so give it a few washes before judging the tone.
Adjustments
Message me first if something isn't right. Prompt adjustments to the original service are free; a change of mind is a new service. Following the home care I recommend keeps this valid.

Aftercare

Typical timings. I'll confirm yours at your appointment. After colour correction, follow the plan we made together.

Balayage & highlights
  1. First few daysWait a couple of days before the first wash. Judge the tone after a few washes.
  2. Every washSulphate-free, colour-safe shampoo, cooler water, heat protectant before hot tools.
  3. Weeks 4–6Toner softens. Purple or blue shampoo once or twice a week, not every wash.
  4. Next visitHighlights about every 6–8 weeks; balayage about every 3–4 months.
Toner & gloss
  1. Every washSulphate-free shampoo and cooler water help the tone last.
  2. Next visitRefresh between colour appointments when brassiness returns.
All-over colour & roots
  1. Every washSulphate-free shampoo and cooler water. In summer, UV protection helps the shade last.
  2. Next visitRoot touch-up about every 4–6 weeks.
Keratin smoothing
  1. First few daysGo gently: no tight ponytails, clips or tucking hair behind your ears.
  2. Every washSulphate-free shampoo only. Sulphates and salt water wear smoothing down faster.
  3. Next visitWhen frizz starts to come back.
Hair botox
  1. Every washSulphate-free shampoo and a rich conditioner.
  2. Once a weekA deep-conditioning mask.
Haircut
  1. Every dayHeat protectant before hot tools; detangle from the ends up.
  2. Next visitTrim about every 6–10 weeks.
