The Girl Behind the Chair

Seven years of colour, cuts, treatments, and brides.

I trained formally in India, spent four years in a full-service salon outside Delhi where I learned the discipline: 25-client weeks, every bride's family watching over my shoulder, every strand counted. I relocated to Canada in 2020.

I'm happiest in the finish. A bridal look can be complicated on paper and still feel off if the braid isn't balanced, the curls aren't sealed, the flowers aren't placed with intention. The difference between a good bridal style and one a bride keeps talking about is almost always the last ten minutes.

I specialize in South Asian bridal: waterfall and fishtail braids, rose-bun architecture, floral placement with gypsophila and fresh blooms, and the dimensional curl work that carries a look from the pheras to the reception. Western bridal, balayage, precision cutting, and smoothing treatments are home too. Every girl deserves to leave the chair feeling more like herself.

The Menu

What I'm known for.

Hair, makeup, brows. Set like a menu because that's what it is: pick what the day calls for, from bridal day-of to everyday upkeep.

Dimensional Balayage

the colour

Hand-painted colour with soft grow-out. Natural, sun-kissed, designed to look good eight weeks from now, not just the day you leave the chair.

Precision Cutting

the cut

Blunt bobs, french bobs, clavicle layers, long movement. Dry cuts, point cuts, whatever the hair is actually asking for.

Colour Correction

the rescue

Box-dye recoveries, banding, brass removal, tone fixes. The work nobody wants but somebody has to do beautifully, and gently.

Keratin & Smoothing

the treatment

Keratin treatments, Brazilian blowouts, anti-frizz smoothing. Healthier hair that behaves, minus the harsh formaldehyde shortcuts.

Perms & Curl Sets

the curls

Modern soft perms, spiral sets, and defined ringlets. Volume and movement that lasts for months, shaped to your face and your mornings.

Extensions & Length

the length

Tape-ins, clip-ins, blending, placement, and maintenance. Subtle enough that the consult focuses on the haircut, not the install.

Events & Blowouts

the night

Red carpet, prom, sangeet, engagement parties. Voluminous blowouts, Hollywood waves, and pin-perfect updos that last all night.

South Asian Bridal

the brides

Sikh, Punjabi, Hindu, Muslim: ceremony-specific looks that hold through the pheras, jaimala, and ten cousins asking for photos. Floral placement is half the craft.

Waterfall & Fishtail

the braids

Woven crowns, cascading braids, butterfly fishtails, and the classic half-up that photographs beautifully from every angle. Balanced tension, nothing pulls or falls.

Floral Placement

the flowers

Rose buns built into the hair itself, fresh flower crowns, baby's breath, bougainvillea. Every bloom placed to survive the ceremony.

Bridal Makeup

the makeup

Full bridal looks built around the outfit and ceremony. Airbrush or HD foundation, custom-blended lip, lashes, and the touch-up plan for a 12-hour day.

Soft Glam & Party

the glam

Sangeet, mehndi, engagement, reception. Buildable glam from soft-day to full-glam-night, plus brow and lash work matched to the look.

Brows & Lashes

the brows

Brow shaping, threading, tinting, lamination, and lash extensions for daily wear or one-day strip lashes for events. Every face has its own perfect arch.
