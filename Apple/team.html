Contrast across the full brightness range

Each curve is the measured WCAG contrast for one approach as the background goes from dark to bright, at the current surface and conditions. The dashed line is the active threshold; the vertical marker is where you are now.

iOS 26 Clear (default) iOS 26.1 Tinted (Apple's global fix) Adaptive (our proposal) WCAG threshold

How to read it: we sample the wallpaper behind each glass panel, composite the panel's dark tint over it at its current opacity, add any sunlight veiling glare, then compute the real WCAG ratio against the white text. The argument the chart makes: Clear dips below the line across a wide bright region; Tinted is a single fixed step, so it sits wastefully high on the easy (dark) end yet can still fall short at the bright extreme; only Adaptive tracks just above the line everywhere — glassy where it can be, opaque only where it must be. The low-vision lens raises the bar to AAA 7:1, where the gap between a fixed setting and an adaptive one becomes impossible to miss.
