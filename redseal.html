Don't forget the practical

In BC, certification also requires a separate practical assessment alongside the written exam. Book both in the SkilledTradesBC Portal and keep your hands sharp on live work while you study theory.

6-week plan, about 90 minutes a day

Six days on, one rest day. Each week ends with a mock exam. Check off days as you go and the progress ribbon fills up.

Daily 90-minute block: 15 min flashcard warm-up · 35 min learn the day's topic · 30 min practice questions on it (review every explanation) · 10 min log misses into your review pile.
