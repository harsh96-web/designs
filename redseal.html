6-week plan, harder load

Since 70% is already in hand, this version raises the daily volume and difficulty and ends on two full 120-question simulations. Six days on, one rest. Check off days and the ribbon fills.

Daily block (about 100 minutes): 15 min flashcard warm-up plus your missed pile · 30 min deep-read the day's topic · 40 min hard practice, 25 to 35 questions, review every explanation and redo misses · 5 min log weak areas.
Mock day: sit the weighted mock, then review 100% of wrong answers and clear them from the Retry pile before moving on.
