Sheet A-03 · Method

How it gets built

Four stages, same order every time. You always know where your project is on the drawing board.

  1. STAGE 01

    Survey

    A 20-minute call. You talk, I take measurements: what you sell, who buys it, and the one thing the site has to do.

  2. STAGE 02

    Draft

    I design in the browser, not in static mockups. Within days you're clicking a working draft of your actual site on your actual phone.

  3. STAGE 03

    Build

    Hand-written HTML, CSS and JavaScript. No plugins to update, nothing to break, and pages that load in under a second.

  4. STAGE 04

    Handover

    You get the files, the domain, and the keys. It's yours — no monthly ransom. I stay on call for tweaks and updates.
