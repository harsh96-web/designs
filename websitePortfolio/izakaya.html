The idea

An izakaya is not a restaurant.

It's the third place — after work, before home — where the food arrives when it's ready and the glass never sits empty.

We cook over white charcoal, buy fish the morning we serve it, and keep a sake list our importer says is irresponsible. Come hungry, stay late.
