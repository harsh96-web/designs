How this works

  • Every question is multiple choice with four options and one best answer.
  • The timer runs continuously and turns red for the final stretch. At zero the exam submits automatically.
  • Move with Previous and Next, or open the Navigator to jump to any question.
  • Use Mark for review to flag a question and come back to it before you finish.
  • You get no feedback until you end the exam. Unanswered questions are scored as wrong.
  • After submitting you see a pass or fail result and a breakdown by block, then a full answer review.

This is an independent practice simulator built to resemble a standard computer-based certification exam. It is not the official SkilledTradesBC or Red Seal testing software, and the real interface may look and behave differently. Questions are original study material aligned to the National Occupational Analysis, not real exam questions. Use it to rehearse pacing, navigation, and endurance.

Stress test

Hardcore mode

A deliberately punishing run, harder than the real exam. Built to break you now so exam day feels easy.

  • 150 questions, drawn only from the extreme-difficulty bank, across all blocks.
  • One minute per question (2.5 hours total). No slack.
  • Negative marking: a wrong answer costs half a mark. A blank costs nothing, so guessing can hurt you.
  • Pass bar 85% on the penalized score, which needs about 90% raw accuracy.

The real Red Seal has no time-per-question limit and no penalty for wrong answers. This mode is a challenge, not a realistic prediction of your exam result.
