Ask on WhatsApp
How much will my colour cost?
Starting prices are in the price menu. Colour depends on length, thickness and what's already on your hair, so the easiest way is to WhatsApp me a photo. I'll confirm a clear price before you book, with no surprises at the chair.
How long does an appointment take?
Colour gets a full three-hour chair so nothing is rushed. Big changes, like going from dark to blonde, can take longer or need two visits, and I'll tell you before the day.
I've used box dye or henna. Can you still colour my hair?
Usually, yes. Colour correction is my specialty. Tell me what was used and roughly when, because it changes the plan, and I may do a strand test first. I'll never push your hair further than it can safely go.
Will colouring damage my hair?
Healthy hair is the whole point. I work in stages and use bond-protecting products. Sometimes the honest answer is two appointments instead of one, and I'll tell you if that's the case.
Where are you, and when can I come in?
I take appointments in Surrey and Vancouver on Tuesdays and Wednesdays, with chairs at 10 am, 1 pm and 5 pm. The exact address and parking details come with your booking. For weddings, I travel across the Lower Mainland.
Can we talk in Punjabi or Hindi?
Of course. Consultations can be in English, Punjabi or Hindi, at the chair or on WhatsApp.
What if I need to cancel?
It's free up to 48 hours before your appointment. Later than that, or a no-show, a fee applies to the card on file. See all policies.
