How much will my colour cost?

It depends on length, thickness and your hair's history. Prices are on the services page, and I'll confirm yours before we begin.

I've used box dye or henna. Can you still colour it?

Usually, yes. Tell me what was used and when, because it changes the plan. I may do a strand test first.

What if I need to cancel?

Give at least 48 hours' notice to cancel or reschedule, and your deposit is refunded or moved to your new appointment. Cancelling within 48 hours forfeits the deposit. Full details are in the policies.
